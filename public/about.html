Our story

Building trust since 2010

Aman Pharma was founded with a simple vision: make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for every Ethiopian. What started as a small pharmacy in Addis Ababa has grown into a nationally recognised pharmaceutical brand.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spans 50,000 sq ft and is equipped with the latest technology. Every product goes through rigorous quality checks before reaching our customers.

With a team of 200+ pharmaceutical experts, doctors and support staff, we serve over 50,000 customers across 200+ cities in Ethiopia.
